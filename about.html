How We Started

North Star Bakery began in a small home kitchen right here in a small neighborhood in West Seattle WA. Driven by a want to provide artisional, slow fermented baking methods, the owners spent years perfecting recipies that bring comfort and a warm nostalgic taste back to our local community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We here at North Star Bakery are a community-centered bakery. This is why we purchase as much of our ingredients from local farmers. We aim to get all of our grains, fruits, and baking supplies from local providers within the greater PNW area. We also strive to use no artificial preservatives or corn syrups in our products.
